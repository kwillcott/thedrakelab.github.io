Kim joined the group as a PhD student in Fall 2024. She holds a BSc (Hons) in Mathematics and Economics and an MSc in Mathematics, both from The University of British Columbia, where her MSc research focused on double-diffusive convection. Her current research examines the interactions between the overturning circulation and freshwater input from ice melt. She uses a box model with representations of both the Southern and Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulations (SMOC and AMOC), to better understand the key mechanisms driving these feedbacks. In her free time she enjoys climbing, baking, and testing out new coffees!
