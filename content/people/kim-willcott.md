Kim joined the group as a PhD student in Fall 2024. She holds a BSc (Hons) in Mathematics and Economics and an MSc in Mathematics, both from the University of British Columbia, where her MSc research focused on double-diffusive convection. Her current research examines the interactions between the overturning circulation and freshwater inputs due to ice melt. She uses a box model with both Southern and Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (SMOC and AMOC) representation to better understand the key mechanisms behind these feedbacks. In her free time she enjoys climbing, baking, and testing out new coffees!
